# Lemma A.2: Second logarithmic variation

**Status: Complete.**

Second derivative of the actual probability-measure logarithmic potential, with exactly the stated c0,c1 coefficients. The negative inverse-Cauchy branch, positive support from the proved edge theorem, log-integral identity, implicit root regularity, root uniqueness, and two differentiations are proved.
