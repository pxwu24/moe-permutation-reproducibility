# Theorem I.1: Nonadditivity for every positive Renyi order

Deduction verified; the block-modified strong-convergence theorem remains an input.

For every real p>0, actual finite-dimensional complex-linear CP/TP channels Phi and conjugate Phi are constructed with strict nonadditivity of their actual minimum output Renyi entropies. The concrete independent Haar ensemble, finite-index choice, channel normalization, antisymmetric postprocessing, spectra, continuity, entropy limits, Bell witness bound, and conjugate-minimum equality are all proved. The only external input is CanonicalStrongInput, the permitted strong-convergence theorem on that explicit ensemble.

The full unverified input and the Lean progress are stated in [partial_progress/](../partial_progress/README.md).
