# Lemma C.2: Scalar certificate for the largest eigenvalue

**Status: Complete.**

The arbitrary-dimension Lemma C.2 implication for the actual Lam body, including derived positive mass under k^2*t>1, is checked; q_paper_formula identifies the paper radicand. This is stronger than the paper because 0<L<1 is unnecessary.
