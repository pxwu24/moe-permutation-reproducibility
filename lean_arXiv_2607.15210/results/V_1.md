# Theorem V.1: Single-output and Bell-output asymptotics after antisymmetric postprocessing

Deduction verified; the block-modified strong-convergence theorem remains an input.

Both actual fixed-k entropy limits and both actual large-k entropy expansions are assembled for the genuine CP/TP antisymmetric postprocessed channels. Fixed-k existence holds for every k>=r with k^2*t>1. The actual body minimum and actual postprocessed Bell matrix have the stated coefficients, with stronger O(k^-5/2) and O(k^-3) errors respectively, for all fixed p>0 including p=1.

The full unverified input and the Lean progress are stated in [partial_progress/](../partial_progress/README.md).

## Assumptions and dependencies

- Fixed integer r>=1,t in (0,1),p>0; for each k>=r with k^2*t>1 take n->infinity first, then k->infinity.
- In the final Lean wrappers, parameter r represents the paper particle count r+1, covering every positive integer particle count. The weighted Bell spectrum is used only in the eventually valid range k>=2(r+1).

Paper dependencies: III.1, IV.1, V.2, B.1, B.2.
