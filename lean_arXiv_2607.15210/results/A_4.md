# Proposition A.4: Asymptotic second Choi moment

Deduction verified; the block-modified strong-convergence theorem remains an input.

The almost-sure limit of the actual normalized full Choi purity is the exact rational coefficient alpha(k,t), for the actual inverse-square-root local normalizer. It follows by summing the proved actual mixed moments.

The full unverified input and the Lean progress are stated in [partial_progress/](../partial_progress/README.md).
